Privacy Policy

When you create a Strategic X account, we collect information such as your full name, email address, and password. Vendors may provide additional information about their shop and products.

3. Authentication Providers

If you sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information (such as your name and email address) from Google to create or access your Strategic X account. We do not receive your Google password.

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate Strategic X (such as our hosting and authentication provider), or when required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you the platform's services.

6. Your Choices

You may update your account information at any time, or request deletion of your account by contacting the Strategic X team.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including secure authentication practices. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of Strategic X after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to the Strategic X team through the contact options provided on the platform.
